# read toilet dimension for free

a free repo for reading the Toilet Dimension comic. hand-drawn in notebooks, scanned and posted arc by arc, free to read for everyone.

this is just a repo, not a website. there is no reader app here, only the page images.

## how to read

the pages are image files, so you need to download them before reading them. open an arc folder, tap a page, download it, then open it in your gallery. read the pages in order (Page 1, Page 2, Page 3 and so on).

## the story so far

a cursed toilet with two buttons, L and R. click R and you get pulled into another dimension. click L and the people trapped there come back. every arc goes further down the hole.

## arcs

- Arc 1: a man clicks R and gets pulled into a tornado
- Arc 2: a skeptical cop checks out the cursed toilet rumors
- Arc 3: 100,000,000 toilets show up in homes and the city goes silent
- Arc 4: The No Fandom Dimension
- Arc 5: the nuke, the black market and the courtroom
- Arc 6: The No Fandom Dimension comes back, Zone 2 appears, and keanenews.com shows up as fake news
- Arc 7: More Dimensions, a melting figure, and a letter asking what you saw in the dimension
- Arc 8: started, more dimensions are on the way
