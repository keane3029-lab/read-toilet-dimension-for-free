# read toilet dimension for free

a free, mobile-first reader for the Toilet Dimension comic. hand-drawn in notebooks, scanned and posted arc by arc, free to read for everyone.

## the story so far

a cursed toilet with two buttons, L and R. click R and you get pulled into another dimension. click L and the people trapped there come back. every arc goes further down the hole.

## arcs

- Arc 1: a man clicks R and gets pulled into a tornado
- Arc 2: a skeptical cop checks out the cursed toilet rumors
- Arc 3: 100,000,000 toilets show up in homes and the city goes silent
- Arc 4: The No Fandom Dimension
- Arc 5: the nuke, the black market and the courtroom
- Arc 6: coming next, the No Fandom Dimension returns

## status

work in progress. the notebook pages are being scanned and turned into a swipe-through reader with a rating button for each arc.
